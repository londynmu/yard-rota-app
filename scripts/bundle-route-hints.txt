- vendor-react: react, react-dom, react-router-dom — always needed for SPA shell
- vendor-supabase: @supabase/supabase-js — auth and data
- vendor-charts: recharts, echarts, echarts-for-react — heavy; lazy routes should avoid loading until /performance (PerformanceChart is lazy)
- vendor-calendar: react-big-calendar, react-datepicker — calendar flows
- vendor-pdf: jspdf, jspdf-autotable — PDF flows
- vendor-utils: date-fns, framer-motion — widely used utilities / motion

Lazy-loaded route components (see HomePage.jsx) pull additional chunks on navigation; the treemap shows which app modules sit in index-* vs async chunks.
